CORRECTION 2026-08-27 (parent verification)
Two release blockers were found by a fresh reviewer after this report was written:
1. STOP was not latched. Fixed with QuestState.SAFETY_STOP plus a controller safety latch.
2. A rejected Qi Future was cancelled without settlement. Fixed with a bounded cancelAndSettle barrier.
Also fixed: Listen timeout data race, missing Dutch Listen locale, duplicate class tap cancelling result
speech, lifecycle status written off the main thread, and the merged-manifest privacy claim below.

contain INTERNET from QiSDK and an exported ProfileInstallReceiver. Both are now removed and verified
against the built APK. Fresh evidence lives in specs/testReport.md.
